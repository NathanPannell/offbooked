Offbooked

Run lines with your whole cast, on your own time.

Each actor gets a link, records their lines from their phone in a few minutes, and everyone gets the full play back as one run-through, with gaps where you speak your part.

How it works

  1. The director uploads the script.Offbooked sorts it into characters, scenes and lines.
  2. Everyone gets their own link.No accounts and no app to install. Open it, tap record, say your line.
  3. The cast hears the whole play.Every take is stitched into one run-through, synced to the script like song lyrics.
